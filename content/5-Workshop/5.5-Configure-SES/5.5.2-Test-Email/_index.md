### Test Email Notification

After completing the Amazon SNS configuration and integrating the Backend, create or update an appointment in the system to verify the email notification feature.

If the configuration is correct, the subscribed email address will receive a notification email sent by the **Dental Clinic Management System**.

![Email Notification](/FCJ_Group_5_AE_C6/images/5-Workshop/5.1-Workshop-overview/nhangmail.png)

The successful delivery of the email confirms that Amazon SNS has been configured correctly and is working properly with the Backend.

At this point, the email notification feature of the **Dental Clinic Management System** is fully operational.
