## General Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

Throughout my participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I experienced a professional, friendly, and supportive learning environment. The mentors and program members were always willing to help whenever I encountered difficulties during my learning process and project implementation. This encouraged me to become more confident in exploring AWS cloud technologies.

### 2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team

The mentors provided detailed guidance, answered questions patiently, and shared valuable advice throughout the project development process. The Admin Team was also very responsive in providing learning materials, assisting with program-related issues, and creating a productive learning environment for all participants.

### 3. Relevance to My Major

The internship program was closely related to my major in Information Technology. Through the program, I had the opportunity to apply my knowledge of programming, databases, web application development, and cloud computing by implementing a real-world project on AWS.

### 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

The program provided numerous opportunities to improve both my technical knowledge and soft skills. I gained hands-on experience with AWS services such as **Amazon EC2, Amazon DynamoDB, Amazon S3, Amazon CloudFront, Amazon SNS, Amazon SES, and Amazon CloudWatch**. In addition, I strengthened my self-learning ability, problem-solving skills, teamwork, time management, and technical documentation skills.

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

The aspect that impressed me the most was the collaborative and supportive culture among mentors and participants. Everyone was willing to share their knowledge and discuss technical challenges together. This created a positive learning environment that motivated me to continuously improve my skills.

### 6. Training Program

The learning roadmap was well organized, starting from fundamental AWS concepts and gradually progressing to hands-on labs and a real-world project. This structured approach made it easier to follow the learning process and effectively master AWS services.

## Additional Questions

### What satisfied me the most during the internship?

The most valuable part of this internship was the opportunity to deploy a real-world application on AWS instead of only learning theoretical concepts. Through the **Dental Clinic Management System** project, I gained practical experience in designing, deploying, and managing a cloud-based application while integrating multiple AWS services into a single solution.

### What do I think could be improved?

The program could include more technical sharing sessions that focus on real-world AWS deployment scenarios, cost optimization strategies, security best practices, and troubleshooting techniques. In addition, organizing more interactive Q&A sessions between mentors and participants would help learners gain a deeper understanding of advanced topics.

### Would I recommend this program to my friends?

**Yes.**

I would definitely recommend the **First Cloud AI Journey** program to my friends because it offers a structured learning path, dedicated mentors, and valuable hands-on experience with AWS services through practical projects. It is an excellent opportunity for students who want to build a strong foundation in cloud computing.

## Suggestions and Expectations

* Continue updating the training materials to include the latest AWS services and cloud technologies.
* Organize more hands-on workshops based on real business scenarios.
* Provide additional career-sharing sessions about becoming a Cloud Engineer, DevOps Engineer, or Solutions Architect.
* Increase networking and knowledge-sharing activities between mentors and participants to strengthen collaboration within the community.

After more than three months of participating in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I have gained valuable knowledge and practical experience in AWS cloud computing. The program not only improved my technical skills but also strengthened my ability to learn independently, solve problems, and work within a real software development process.

Completing the **Dental Clinic Management System** project was a significant milestone that gave me greater confidence in developing and deploying cloud-based applications in the future. I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the mentors, the Admin Team, and everyone involved in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program for their continuous guidance, support, and encouragement throughout my internship.
